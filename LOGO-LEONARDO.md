# 🎨 Prompt Leonardo AI — Logo SteFi Tournoi FC 26

## Prompt principal (colle-le dans Leonardo)

```
Professional esports tournament logo for "SteFi Tournoi FC 26", soccer / football video game championship. 
Shield or crest shape. 
Central element: a soccer ball with hexagonal pattern, glowing neon green (#00FF87), surrounded by electric sparks.
Color palette: neon green (#00FF87) and electric violet (#A855F7) on deep dark navy background (#0D1B2A).
Typography: "STEFI" in bold condensed uppercase font at the top of the shield, "FC 26" smaller below the ball.
Style: modern gaming / esports aesthetic, metallic gradients, subtle lens flare, cinematic lighting, premium badge design.
Background: transparent or dark navy.
No people, no players, no faces.
Square format, centered composition.
Ultra-detailed, 4K, vector-clean edges.
```

---

## ⚙️ Paramètres recommandés dans Leonardo

| Paramètre | Valeur |
|---|---|
| **Model** | Leonardo Kino XL ou SDXL Lightning |
| **Preset Style** | Illustration ou Graphic Design |
| **Width × Height** | 1024 × 1024 (carré) |
| **Guidance Scale** | 7–8 |
| **Steps** | 30–40 |
| **Number of images** | 4 (pour choisir) |

## 🚫 Negative Prompt (ajoute-le dans "Negative Prompt")

```
realistic photo, people, faces, players, crowd, stadium interior, 
blurry, low quality, text errors, watermark, ugly, deformed, 
extra elements, busy background, white background
```

## 💡 Après génération

1. Choisis la meilleure image
2. Utilise **"Remove Background"** dans Leonardo ou sur remove.bg
3. Exporte en **PNG transparent 1024×1024**
4. Dans l'app SteFi Tournoi → Admin → **Importer un logo**
